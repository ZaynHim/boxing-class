The road to 50–0

  1. 1996

    Olympic bronze

    Wins bronze at the Atlanta Olympics after a controversial semi-final loss, then turns pro the same year.

  2. 1998

    First world title

    Beats Genaro Hernández to become WBC super featherweight champion at 21.

  3. 2007

    Beats Oscar De La Hoya

    Wins a split decision in one of the biggest pay-per-view events boxing had ever seen.

  4. 2015

    Fight of the century

    Defeats Manny Pacquiao by unanimous decision in the best-selling pay-per-view in boxing history.

  5. 2017

    Fight number 50

    Stops UFC star Conor McGregor in round 10 and passes Rocky Marciano's 49–0 record.

How he fought

His whole game was built around one idea: hit and don't get hit.

Shoulder roll

Lead shoulder high, chin tucked behind it. Right hands roll off the shoulder instead of landing.

Pull counter

Leans back just out of range as the jab comes, then fires a right hand straight back.

Ring control

Uses footwork to keep fights at his distance, turning opponents so they are always reset.

Conditioning

Known for late-night runs and long gym sessions, so he was often strongest in the late rounds.

Strange but true

Click a question to see the answer.

How long was the longest boxing match with gloves?

110 rounds. Andy Bowen and Jack Burke fought for over seven hours in 1893. The referee called it a no contest because both were too tired to continue.

Why is it called a ring if it's a square?

Early fights took place inside a circle drawn on the ground, with the crowd standing around it. The square came later, but the name stayed.

When did boxers start wearing gloves?

The Marquess of Queensberry Rules of 1867 made gloves and three-minute rounds standard. Before that, most fights were bare-knuckle.

How old is boxing as a sport?

Boxing joined the ancient Greek Olympic Games in 688 BC. Fighters wrapped their hands in leather straps.

Do Olympic boxers still wear headgear?

Not the men. Headgear was dropped for elite men's Olympic boxing starting at the 2016 Rio Games.

What happened in Mayweather's first pro fight?

He knocked out Roberto Apodaca in the second round in October 1996. It was the start of the 50–0 run.
