Questions

The stuff everyone asks before their first class.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Come in, train with a coach and see if the gym fits you. No payment and no commitment.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can move to a different plan when your current one ends. Just tell us at the front desk.

Can kids join?

Yes. We have a kids class for ages 8 to 15, focused on technique, fitness and discipline.

How do I pay?

You pay at the gym on your first day after the free class, in cash or by card.
