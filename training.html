Questions

Do I need any experience?

No. Boxing Fundamentals is made for people who have never thrown a punch. Most of our members started there.

What should I bring?

Sports clothes, training shoes, a towel and water. We give you hand wraps, and gloves are available at the gym.

Will I have to spar?

Only if you want to. Sparring starts at the advanced level, and always with a coach watching.

How do I know my level?

Book a free class and a coach will watch you train, then tell you which course to start with.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. The 3 month and yearly plans can be frozen once for up to two weeks.
